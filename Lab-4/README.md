## Implemented Features

1. Fixed player movement boundaries to prevent the player from moving outside the screen.
2. Implemented dynamic difficulty scaling, increasing anvil speed and spawn frequency as survival time increases.
3. Added speed-based warning colors for falling anvils.
4. Added ground impact particle effects and screen shake when anvils hit the ground.
